Thresholds (your decision): a drop of at least 0.30 on the 0-3 judge scale (or the measured judge noise, if larger) or 10 percentage points on a rate, with the same sign in at least 4 paired repetitions and larger than the spread of the full variant; reconstruct agreement (kappa) at least 0.60; ordering tau-b at least 0.90.

Limits: one skill (test-audit), 42 lessons, one model, `claude -p` mode, 5 paired repetitions (a sign test cannot reach p < 0.05 with 5 pairs, so the gate is an effect size), a biased gold (3 planned lessons chosen with the scores visible), a judge that is itself a model, and lexical information measures. Static evidence alone never gives more than low confidence.
